What it does

Everything your vehicle papers need

Your whole garage at a glance

A gauge counts down to your next renewal. Road-ready lights show which vehicles can legally leave home, and everything expired or due soon is listed first.

Every paper, with days left

Each vehicle is shown by its own number plate — white, yellow or green, as on the road. Every document shows how many days it has left, and how much of its validity is used up.

The document itself, when you need it

Snap a document with the camera or pick it from your gallery. When someone asks to see your insurance, it's two taps away — stored encrypted, next to its dates.

Reminders that actually arrive

  • 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before expiry, and on the day.
  • At the time you choose.
  • Scheduled on your phone, so they work even if you never open the app.
  • Settings shows exactly how many reminders are set.

Private, with a backup only you can open

Everything is encrypted on your phone, with no account and no cloud. To move phones, make a backup file protected by a recovery code only you hold. Keep it on Drive, in email, or on a computer.

Light or dark

Follows your phone's theme, or pick one in Settings. The instrument panel looks the same in both, so the readings that matter never change.

How it works

From first vehicle to first reminder

Step 1 of 5

Add your vehicle

  1. Open Vehicles and tap Add vehicle.
  2. Type the registration number — spaces and dashes don't matter.
  3. Pick car, two-wheeler or commercial. Make, model and the rest are optional.

Step 2 of 5

Add its papers

  1. Open the vehicle and tap Add document.
  2. Choose RC, Insurance, PUC or FASTag.
  3. Take photo or Choose from gallery.
  4. Set the expiry date and save. Reminders are set up straight away.

Step 3 of 5

See where you stand

Each vehicle shows whether it's road-ready and how many days every document has left. Home sums it all up with a gauge to the next renewal.

Step 4 of 5

Get reminded

You'll hear from VehicleVault 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before anything expires, and on the day. Set the time in Settings, and send yourself a test notification to be sure.

On Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo or Vivo? Allow it to run in the background.

Step 5 of 5

Back it up

Settings → Backup → Back up now. Write down your recovery code and save the file somewhere off the phone. If your phone is ever lost, that's how your papers come back.
