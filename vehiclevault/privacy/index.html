VehicleVault · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

VehicleVault stores your vehicle documents — details, dates and photos — on your phone only. They are encrypted on the device. We have no servers that hold them, and we cannot see them.

This policy covers the VehicleVault app for Android, published by AppCraft Solutions ("we").

What leaves your phone

  • Crash reports (on by default; turn off in Settings → Privacy): the technical details of a crash — app version, device model, Android version, and where in the code it failed — sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics. They never include your documents, vehicle numbers, dates or photos.
  • Usage statistics (off by default; turn on in Settings → Privacy): anonymous counts, such as how many documents are added and of which type, sent to Google Firebase Analytics. Never your documents, vehicle numbers, dates or photos. No advertising ID is collected.

Both use a random identifier for this installation — not your name, phone number or Google account. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

What stays on your phone

Your vehicles, documents, dates, notes and photos. They're stored in an encrypted database and encrypted files, with keys held in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). They are excluded from Android's own cloud backup and device transfer.

Backups

Backups are files you create and send where you choose, such as Google Drive, email or a computer. They're encrypted with a recovery code that only you hold. We never receive them, and we can't open them.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to remind you before documents expire. This is the only permission the app asks for.
  • Camera and photos — the app uses Android's own camera and photo picker, so it never gets access to your camera or your photo library. It only receives the single photo you take or pick.
  • Internet — used only for crash reports and usage statistics, as described above.

Children

VehicleVault is meant for vehicle owners aged 18 and over, and isn't directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone. To have crash or usage data deleted, email support@appcraft.solutions — we'll delete it from Firebase. Turning both settings off in Settings → Privacy stops any further collection.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
